Privacy Policy for FeedRelay

Effective date: 9 October 2026

This policy covers the FeedRelay app published on Google Play with package name com.mandos.feedrelay.intl. FeedRelay is an RSS reader. It has no account and no developer server.

Information stored on your device

Feeds you add, articles, read and saved state, categories, tags, and settings stay in the app’s storage on your phone. That includes an API key if you choose to enter one. The developer does not receive this information.

A backup you export is a file you save yourself. That file can include your settings and API key. Android backup, if you have it turned on, can also include the app’s data.

Information sent when you use the app

To load a feed, article, or image, the app contacts the site that published it. That site, and services it uses, can see your IP address, the same way a browser would. Source icons may also be requested from the site itself or from Google’s favicon service.

The app does not include advertising or analytics, and it does not sell your information.

Optional AI features

Summaries, translation, and AI ranking of the daily digest are off until you turn them on and enter your own API key. The key stays on your device. When you use one of these features, the app sends the request from your phone to the provider you selected:

  • OpenAI
  • Anthropic (Claude)
  • Google (Gemini)
  • Groq

A summary or translation sends the article text. Digest ranking sends article titles only. The provider processes that request under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive the text or the reply.

Read aloud

The phone’s own speech engine keeps the text on the device. If you choose the online voice, the article text is sent to Google with your Gemini API key so it can make the audio.

Donations

A donation is an optional purchase through Google Play. Google processes the payment. The app does not receive your card number or payment details. Google Play’s privacy policy applies to that purchase.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows them on your device for new articles, the daily digest, or sync problems. Those notifications are created on the phone.

Children

FeedRelay is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
